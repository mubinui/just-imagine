# Animal sound provenance

Recordings used by the Animals world packs in `packages/learning-content`. Only CC0 or public-domain recordings are allowed. Before each file was downloaded, its licence was checked on the file's own page. Files live in `packages/learning-content/assets/sounds/`, and each pack's `sounds[].provenance` repeats the source, licence, URL and author.

Sourced 2026-10-16. All twelve animals have a recording; none are missing.

## Processing

- For each recording longer than 4 seconds, the 3.6-second window with the highest energy was chosen automatically. Shorter files were kept whole.
- Each clip was mixed to mono, given a 50 ms fade-in and a 250 ms fade-out, loudness-normalised to about -18 LUFS with -2 dBTP true peak, and resampled to 44.1 kHz.
- Each clip was encoded as Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz, quality 4. The FFmpeg build on the authoring machine had no `libvorbis`, and FFmpeg's built-in Vorbis encoder only writes two channels. Every file is therefore dual-mono: one mono signal copied identically to both channels. To make true mono files, re-encode with `libvorbis` (`-ac 1 -c:a libvorbis`).
- Every file is under 66 KB and at most 3.6 seconds long, inside the 200 KB and 4 s limits.
- The freesound sources (monkey, seal, penguin) were taken from freesound's public high-quality MP3 previews, because original downloads need an account. The licence is the same CC0 dedication as the original.

## Notes for content review

- The animal clips were picked automatically and nobody has listened to them yet. They need a listening check before sign-off. Watch the cow clip: it comes from a herd recording, so cowbells may be louder than the moo.
- The seal recording is a sea lion, which belongs to the seal family. The Seal character says that its sea lion cousins bark loudly.
- Commons had no CC0 or public-domain recordings for monkey, seal or penguin, so those three came from freesound.org. Every candidate on Commons was CC BY or CC BY-SA, and those were rejected.
- The pet sounds referenced in `packages/learning-content/src/pets.ts` (`assets/sounds/pets/*.ogg`) are not animal recordings from this list. They are authored separately and do not exist yet.
