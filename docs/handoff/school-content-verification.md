The previous secondary-source taxonomy incorrectly included a fourteenth Physics chapter and used an older title for chapter 13. Both official language versions agree on 13 chapters. The taxonomy now reflects that edition; existing activity references to Physics 2, 3 and 11 are unchanged. Chemistry lab references 7 and 9, general mathematics 9 and 12, and higher mathematics 8 and 11 match the official order.

Chapter labels are concise interface labels; they are not reproduced textbook prose. The seven demo lessons are original bilingual instructional material. Simulations are idealized teaching models, and the chemistry bench states those limits in its interface. Equation balance and deterministic calculations are tested. This source check does not claim NCTB endorsement or an independent science teacher's review of every explanation or AI prediction.
