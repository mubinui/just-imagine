## Motion thesis

Focal moments are making and storytelling: specialists actually manipulate the tools of their subject; story visuals reveal the narrated action. Continuity follows scene/beat and build milestones. Game feedback shows the consequence of a legal move, collected object, connected route or completed assembly. Animation is bounded, pauses hidden/offscreen, and has a static reduced-motion equivalent. No independent random drifting, endless whole-image rocking or decorative particle fields.
