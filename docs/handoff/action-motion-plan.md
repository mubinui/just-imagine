The user rejected whole-image sliding and random wandering. Running must have strides, wheels must turn and swimming must have propulsion and direction.

## Research and implementation

- `packages/engine/src/renderer.ts` translated whole sprites; `simulation.ts` already supplied physical poses but no grounded presentation contract. `CinematicArt.tsx` had conversation/celebration poses, while `AdventureBoard.tsx` moved logical tile positions immediately. The builder generated single images. Preserve the existing engine, authored stories, child-directed creation and all teammate changes.
- `engine/src/motion.ts` now derives gait phase/facing/swim pitch and signed wheel travel from actual displacement. Stops freeze the cycle; invalid clock gaps and teleports do not spin through frames. Physical locomotion actors remain upright, with dynamic grounded state preventing running during falls. Renderer caches frame textures and releases them on replacement/disposal. Old worlds without metadata retain their existing art/physics.
- Optional scene `locomotion` and asset `animation` metadata are validated and preserved through protocol/API storage. Generated run/walk/swim/fly actors get a nine-frame, three-by-three sheet. Vehicles may specify mechanical wheel pivots/radii. Generation instructions require intentional routes/input, stopping and coherent ground/water behavior. A stride-only adjustment reuses owned artwork; a new action/facing/rig regenerates it. Missing/failed owned artwork is eligible for regeneration on an edit.
- `processMotionAtlas` finds actual transparent gutter valleys, removes animation key-color fringe, rejects clipped backgrounds and multiple major silhouettes in a frame, applies one shared scale, and registers runners to the ground or swimmers/flyers to their body center. Existing single-image processing is unchanged. Safety/matching review remains mandatory. Animation cache version is `motion-v2`.
- Authored animal atlas originals came from built-in imagegen. The first layout crossed nominal cell boundaries; a targeted generation edit still required deterministic alpha-silhouette registration. `apps/web/scripts/normalize-locomotion.mjs` extracts complete connected silhouettes, preserves one scale per character and builds final six-by-four atlases. Three sheets contain72poses for12animals. Final assets are `animals-{farm,jungle,ocean}-locomotion-v2.{png,webp}`; raw sources, exact prompts and registration measurements are in `docs/art/`.
- `useTravel` follows queued legal destinations with acceleration/braking, preserves corners and pauses hidden-tab gaps. The rules commit a move or delivery on arrival. Lion uses run poses, Seal and Dolphin swim with strokes/pitch/facing, Horse advances with correct hoofbeat progress, and the Mars rover's spokes turn by signed distance divided by12pxradius. Resting stops; no ambient movement loop was added.
